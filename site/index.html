Worth knowing now

Two things change on 31 March 2027

—VAT goes back onSolar panels, heat pumps and home batteries fitted in a home are VAT-free until 31 March 2027. From 1 April the reduced rate applies.
—The £9,000 grant endsHomes on oil or LPG can get £9,000 off an air or ground source heat pump until then, instead of the usual £7,500.

Sources: HMRC and the Energy Saving Trust.

Before you get quotes

The questions people ask first

Short answers. Each guide goes further and links to the official source.

How much is the heat pump grant?

£7,500 off an air, ground or water source heat pump through the Boiler Upgrade Scheme in England and Wales, or £9,000 for homes on oil or LPG until 31 March 2027. Your installer applies for it and takes it off the quote.

Is there VAT on solar panels, batteries and heat pumps?

Not until 31 March 2027. Installing them in a home in Great Britain is zero-rated, and the reduced rate applies from 1 April 2027.

Do I need an MCS-certified installer?

For the heat pump grant, yes. For solar, MCS certification is what most energy suppliers ask for before they’ll pay you for the electricity you export.

How does Wattwell make money?

From spring 2027, installers pay a fee when a homeowner asks us to pass on their details. You never pay, and nobody can pay for a better write-up.
