For installers

A free listing, and real introductions

Wattwell’s directory of Sussex and Kent installers opens in March 2027.

The listing is free

We build your listing from your website and the public MCS register, then email you to confirm it. You can correct it at any time. Nobody can pay for a better write-up, or to appear higher in a list.

Introductions, from spring 2027

When a homeowner asks us to pass their details to installers in their area, they choose to, and we pass them on with their permission. You pay a fee only for those introductions: no subscription and no contract. We’ll agree the fee with installers before the first introduction is sent.

What we’ll need

  • Your MCS certificate number and the technologies it covers.
  • Your consumer code membership (RECC or HIES).
  • The areas you cover, the work you take on (including rental properties), your usual lead times and warranties.

To be included, email hello@wattwell.co.uk with your company name and website.
