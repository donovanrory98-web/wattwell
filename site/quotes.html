Quotes from installers we’ve checked

Free for you, and only ever with your permission.

Quotes open in March 2027, when the first installers are listed. Until then, nothing on this page collects your details.

How it will work

  1. You tell us what you’re thinking about (a heat pump, solar, a battery), your postcode and how to reach you.
  2. You choose to let us pass your details to up to three installers who cover your area. Every one of them will have been checked: certificate, consumer code and their own confirmed details.
  3. The installers contact you directly with quotes. You decide whether to go ahead, and with whom.

What it costs

Nothing for you. Each installer pays Wattwell a fee for an introduction you’ve asked us to make. That’s how the guides stay free. Paying doesn’t buy an installer a better write-up, and we never arrange finance.

In the meantime

Read the heat pump, solar and battery guides for the questions to ask, and use GOV.UK’s installer search to find certified installers near you.
