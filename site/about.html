About

An honest guide to home energy

Wattwell is a small, independent guide to heat pumps, solar panels and home batteries for Sussex and Kent.

How we check installers

  1. The certificate: each installer’s MCS certificate, looked up on the public register.
  2. The consumer code: membership of RECC or HIES.
  3. Their own details: areas, jobs, lead times and warranties, confirmed by the installer in writing.
  4. The date: every listing shows when it was last checked.

How Wattwell is paid

  • Homeowners never pay. The guides and quotes are free.
  • Installers pay for introductions, from spring 2027, and only when a homeowner has asked us to pass on their details.
  • A listing is free, and money never changes what we say about an installer or the order they appear in.
  • No finance, no insurance. We never arrange or recommend either.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t recommend or guarantee any installer, or their work.
  • We give general information, never advice on a particular home.
