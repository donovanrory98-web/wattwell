Heat pumps, without the jargon

A heat pump moves heat from the air or the ground into your home. It can replace a gas, oil or LPG boiler, and there’s £7,500 off the price.

How a heat pump works

An air source heat pump sits outside, a bit like an air-conditioning unit, and draws warmth from the air, even on a cold day. It uses electricity to raise that warmth to a useful temperature for your radiators and hot water. A ground source pump does the same from pipes buried in the garden.

Because it runs cooler than a boiler, a good installation starts with a room-by-room heat loss survey. That tells the installer how big the pump should be, and whether any radiators need to be larger.

£7,500 off, claimed by your installer

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme takes £7,500 off an air, ground or water source heat pump in England and Wales. Until 31 March 2027, homes heated by oil or LPG can get £9,000 instead. You don’t fill in a form: your installer applies and takes the grant off the quote.

  • You need to own the home, and be replacing fossil fuel heating, or electric heating that isn’t a heat pump.
  • The installer must be MCS-certified and belong to a consumer code such as RECC or HIES.

VAT-free until March 2027

Fitting a heat pump in a home in Great Britain is zero-rated for VAT until 31 March 2027. From 1 April 2027 the reduced rate applies, so if you’re close to deciding, the date is worth knowing.

Five questions for every quote

  1. Heat loss: will you do a room-by-room heat loss survey, and can I see it?
  2. Radiators: what flow temperature is the system designed for, and which radiators need changing?
  3. Certification: what’s your MCS certificate number, and which consumer code do you belong to?
  4. The grant: will you apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme for me, and is the price shown after the grant?
  5. Aftercare: what does the warranty cover, for how long, and who services it?

Common questions

Can a landlord get the heat pump grant?

The scheme is for owners of the property, including landlords who own it, who are replacing fossil fuel or non-heat-pump electric heating. Your installer checks eligibility when they apply.

Do I apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme myself?

No. Your MCS-certified installer applies and takes the grant off your quote.

When does VAT-free fitting end?

On 31 March 2027. The reduced rate applies from 1 April 2027.
