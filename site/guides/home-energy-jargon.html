The words on your quote, explained

Quotes for heat pumps, solar and batteries come with their own language. Here it is in plain English.

TermWhat it means
kW (kilowatt)Power: how much energy something uses or makes at one moment.
kWh (kilowatt hour)Energy over time, and the unit on your electricity bill. A 1 kW heater left on for an hour uses 1 kWh.
kWp (kilowatt peak)The most a solar system makes in full sun. Ask for the yearly figure in kWh too.
InverterThe box that turns the direct current from panels or a battery into the alternating current your home uses.
Usable capacityHow much of a battery’s stored energy you can actually use, in kWh. Often a little less than the headline size.
SCOPSeasonal coefficient of performance: roughly how many units of heat a heat pump gives for each unit of electricity, averaged over a year. Higher is better.
Flow temperatureHow hot the water going to your radiators is. Heat pumps are most efficient at lower flow temperatures, which is why radiator sizes matter.
Heat loss surveyA room-by-room estimate of how much heat your home loses, used to size a heat pump and its radiators.
MCSThe Microgeneration Certification Scheme, which certifies installers and products. Needed for the heat pump grant, and usually for export payments.
RECC and HIESConsumer codes for home energy installers. The Boiler Upgrade Scheme needs your installer to belong to one.
Boiler Upgrade SchemeThe government grant of £7,500 towards a heat pump in England and Wales (£9,000 for oil and LPG homes until 31 March 2027).
Smart Export GuaranteePayments from your energy supplier for the solar electricity you send back to the grid.
EPCEnergy Performance Certificate: rates a home from A, the most efficient, to G. Rentals must reach C by 1 October 2030.

Common questions

What’s the difference between kW and kWh?

kW is power at one moment. kWh is energy used or made over time, the unit on your bill.

What is a good SCOP for a heat pump?

Higher is better: it’s roughly the units of heat you get for each unit of electricity over a year. Compare quotes designed to the same temperature.

What does MCS stand for?

The Microgeneration Certification Scheme, which certifies installers and products for solar, batteries and heat pumps.
