The £7,500 grant, step by step

The Boiler Upgrade Scheme takes money off a heat pump or biomass boiler in England and Wales. Your installer applies for it, so you never handle the grant yourself.

How much you can get

What you’re fittingGrant
Air source heat pump£7,500
Ground or water source heat pump£7,500
Air or ground source, in a home on oil or LPG (21 July 2026 to 31 March 2027)£9,000
Air-to-air heat pump£2,500
Biomass boiler (rural homes off the gas grid)£5,000

Who can get it

  • Owners of homes and small businesses in England and Wales.
  • You need to be replacing fossil fuel heating, such as a gas, oil or LPG boiler, or electric heating that isn’t a heat pump.

How it works

  1. Get quotes from MCS-certified installers who belong to a consumer code (RECC or HIES). The Energy Saving Trust suggests at least three.
  2. The installer you choose applies for the grant for you.
  3. The grant comes off your quote, and you pay the rest.

The installer confirms eligibility for your home when they apply. Wattwell gives general information only.

Common questions

How do I apply for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme?

You don’t: your MCS-certified installer applies and takes the grant off your quote.

When does the £9,000 grant end?

The higher grant for homes on oil or LPG runs until 31 March 2027. After that the standard grant is £7,500.

Can I get the grant for a new build?

The scheme is for replacing existing fossil fuel or non-heat-pump electric heating, so ask your installer.
